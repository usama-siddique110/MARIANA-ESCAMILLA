Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Applications & Digital Services

Effective Date: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MARIANA ESCAMILLA LLC ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use our mobile applications, digital platforms, and software services ("Services").

About Our Services

Our applications and digital products are developed and maintained by MARIANA ESCAMILLA LLC. They are designed for seamless performance, entertainment, and utility, and do not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Information We Collect

We prioritize user privacy: we do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

Our applications do not require:

  • User registration or sign-up
  • Login or authentication credentials
  • Access to contacts or address books
  • Access to precise device location
  • Access to camera or microphone
  • Access to private photos, documents, or personal files
  • Any personally identifiable information from users

Offline & Edge Functionality

Our offline-capable applications are designed to run securely on-device without continuous external server communication. We do not operate unnecessary tracking telemetry or profile users.

Third-Party Services

Our standard applications do not integrate invasive third-party ad networks or surveillance frameworks that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain any personal information from children under the age of 13 or any other users.

Data Security

Because we operate on a strict data-minimization architecture and do not collect or store user data, there is no personal information stored or maintained by MARIANA ESCAMILLA LLC through these applications.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page, and the Effective Date will be updated accordingly.
